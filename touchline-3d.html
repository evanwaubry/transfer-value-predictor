How the estimates are calculated

The repository uses standardized goals, assists, appearances, and age, plus one-hot encoded position, with Ridge regression (alpha 10). Each validation fold fits preprocessing on its training rows only. All observations of a player remain in the same fold. The player cards show held-out estimates; the simulator uses a final model fitted on all 40 profiles. Negative predictions are clipped to zero.

The baseline uses training-fold position medians. Mean absolute error is an average across players, not an individual confidence interval. Contract duration, xG/xA, minutes, injuries, and defensive or goalkeeping performance are not model inputs. The simulator's contract adjustment is explicitly illustrative. The demo's club labels reflect its supplied snapshot.
